# Reliability And Residual Structure

This subgroup contains follow-ups on measurement structure after the main
reliability checks in `02_alignment_reliability/`. The analyses ask how much
brain-response variance remains unexplained, whether residual structure is
itself reliable, and whether conclusions degrade gracefully under restricted or
noisier settings.

- `residual_reliability/`: reliability of residual brain geometry after model
  structure is accounted for.
- `unexplained_variance/`: variance left outside the modeled alignment signal.
- `graceful_degradation/`: sensitivity checks under reduced information or
  degraded settings.
